# Product Goals — PokéRescue Sentinel

## Primary Goals
1. Provide a reliable, high-performance desktop interface for emergency coordination on Windows.
2. Maintain strict data provenance: clear distinction between verified passable roads, stale status, and unverified reports.
3. Protect responder safety by preventing premature dispatch onto hazardous corridors.
4. Keep experimental sensing research fully decoupled from operational rescue dispatching.

## Explicit Non-Goals
- No unverified autonomous dispatching without human commander approval.
- No public mass tracking or unauthorized device surveillance.
- No real-time life-safety claims based on uncalibrated experimental Wi-Fi CSI sensing.
- No direct client-side mutation of disaster records without backend validation.
