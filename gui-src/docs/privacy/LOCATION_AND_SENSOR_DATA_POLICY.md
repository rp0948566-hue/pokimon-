# Location and Sensor Data Privacy Policy

## Policies
1. **Authorized Telemetry Only**: Only verified emergency response personnel and official fleet vehicles broadcast location coordinates.
2. **No Civilian Tracking**: The application contains zero civilian tracking, phone beaconing, or mass surveillance capabilities.
3. **Experimental Sensing Privacy**: Experimental Wi-Fi CSI sensing frames contain only synthetic or laboratory RF multipath metrics. No person-level identity or biometric profiles are collected or inferred.
4. **Retention and Expiry**: Operational location updates expire after mission completion and are archived according to municipal emergency management standards.
