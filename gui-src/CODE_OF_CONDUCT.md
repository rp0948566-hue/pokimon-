# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the PokéRescue Sentinel project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or beliefs.

## Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and architectural considerations.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for project quality, safety, and reliability.
